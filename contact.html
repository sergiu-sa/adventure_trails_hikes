04 / Five things people ask first.

If your question isn't here, the inquiry form will get a longer answer back to you within a day.

What's included in the trip price? +

Accommodation, all meals, in-region transport, certified guides, cultural programming, gear-library access, and verified carbon-offset contributions. Excluded: international flights, personal technical kit, travel insurance, and visas.

What fitness level do I need? +

We grade every route T1 to T5. See the spec sheet on the expeditions catalogue. T2 routes suit motivated first-timers; T5 routes require months of altitude conditioning and recent multi-day trekking experience. We'll never sell you above your grade.

How far ahead should I book? +

3–6 months is comfortable. Permit-restricted routes (Everest, Annapurna, Toubkal) need 6–8 months minimum. Cancellation slots open occasionally. We maintain a short-notice list, so ask about it.

Is travel insurance required? +

Mandatory. Coverage must include medical, evacuation (helicopter if applicable), and trip cancellation. We can introduce you to two providers who specialise in adventure travel and don't have small-print exclusions for altitudes above 4,500 m.

Dietary requirements? +

Yes. Vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, halal, kosher: all accommodated when you tell us at booking. We coordinate directly with each host community 4 weeks before departure.
